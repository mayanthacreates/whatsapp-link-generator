Frequently asked questions

What is a wa.me link?

wa.me is WhatsApp's official short-link domain. A link like wa.me/971501234567 opens a chat with that number directly - no need to save the contact first.

Is this free? Do you store my number?

Completely free, no signup. The link and QR code are generated entirely in your browser - your number never leaves your device.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. wa.me links work identically for personal WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business accounts, on both iOS and Android.

What format should my number be in?

Full international format without spaces, dashes or a leading zero - the tool handles this automatically when you pick your country code.

Can I print the QR code?

Yes - the downloaded PNG is crisp at print size for menus, packaging and storefront stickers. For very large formats, get in touch and I'll prepare a vector version.
